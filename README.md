## 📊 Project Overview

Comparative economic analysis of **234 countries** across **20 development indicators**, examining regional disparities and growth opportunities between Africa and The Americas using interactive Tableau visualizations.

**Tools Used:** Tableau, Microsoft Excel  

---

## 🎯 Objectives

1. Analyze economic performance across four key categories:
   - **Health**: Life expectancy, mortality rates, healthcare expenditure
   - **Finance**: GDP, GDP per capita
   - **Infrastructure**: Railways, internet penetration, mobile adoption
   - **Demographics**: Population distribution, urbanization, birth rates

2. Identify regional strengths and weaknesses
3. Compare Africa vs The Americas across development metrics
4. Deliver data-driven policy recommendations

---

## 📈 Key Findings

### **The Americas - Strengths**
- **3x higher GDP per capita** compared to Africa
- **85% internet penetration** vs 40% in Africa
- Higher life expectancy (77 years vs 64 years)
- Advanced digital infrastructure

### **Africa - Strengths**
- **120% growth in mobile adoption** (fastest globally)
- **Younger demographic** (43% population under age 15)
- Emerging digital economy potential
- Rapid urbanization trends

### **Regional Gaps Identified**
- Digital divide: 45 percentage point gap in internet access
- Healthcare expenditure: Americas spend 2.5x more per capita
- Economic inequality: Significant GDP per capita disparities
- Infrastructure development needs in Africa

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

### **For Africa:**
1. **Digital Infrastructure Expansion**: Increase internet connectivity, especially in rural areas
2. **Economic Diversification**: Transition from primary industries to technology and services
3. **FDI Attraction**: Create policies encouraging foreign investment in tech/infrastructure
4. **Education & Skills Development**: Leverage young demographic through training programs
5. **Transportation Infrastructure**: Improve road networks and rail systems

### **For The Americas:**
1. **Digital Inequality Reduction**: Expand broadband access to underserved communities
2. **Sustainable Development**: Focus on green energy and reduce resource dependency
3. **Inclusive Economic Policies**: Address wealth gaps and foster equitable growth
4. **Green Technology Adoption**: Transition to electric vehicles and renewable energy
5. **Healthcare Reform**: Address aging population challenges
